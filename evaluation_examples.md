# AI Evaluation Examples

## Example 1 – Accurate Response

**Prompt:** What is the capital of India?

**AI Response:** The capital of India is New Delhi.

**Evaluation:**  
- Accuracy: 5/5
- Relevance: 5/5
- Completeness: 5/5
- Clarity: 5/5
- Safety: 5/5
- Overall Score: 5/5

**Reason:** The response is factually correct, directly answers the question, and is clear and concise.

---

## Example 2 – Incomplete Response

**Prompt:** What are the benefits of regular exercise?

**AI Response:** Exercise is good for your health.

**Evaluation:**  
- Accuracy: 5/5
- Relevance: 4/5
- Completeness: 2/5
- Clarity: 5/5
- Safety: 5/5
- Overall Score: 4/5

**Reason:** The response is correct but incomplete because it does not explain specific benefits such as improved fitness, strength, and cardiovascular health.

---

## Example 3 – Clear and Relevant Response

**Prompt:** How can I improve my resume?

**AI Response:** Use a clear format, highlight relevant skills and achievements, tailor your resume to the job description, and proofread it before submitting.

**Evaluation:**  
- Accuracy: 5/5
- Relevance: 5/5
- Completeness: 5/5
- Clarity: 5/5
- Safety: 5/5
- Overall Score: 5/5

**Reason:** The response provides practical, relevant, and easy-to-follow recommendations.

## Key Evaluation Skills

- Identifying factual errors
- Checking relevance
- Detecting incomplete responses
- Assessing clarity
- Applying consistent scoring
- Writing evaluation reasons
