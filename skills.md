## Quality Assurance Approach

My evaluation approach focuses on:

1. Understanding the prompt requirements.
2. Checking whether the response answers the prompt.
3. Identifying factual or logical errors.
4. Checking completeness and clarity.
5. Applying consistent scoring criteria.
6. Providing a concise and objective evaluation reason.
